Red Flag Run

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Red Flag Run is a free game that teaches people to spot scams. We built it to collect as little as possible. In short: no accounts, no ads, no tracking.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. We don't run analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we don't sell or share personal data.

What stays on your device

To save your progress, the app stores the following in your browser or phone storage, on your device only:

  • Your progress: XP, streak, shields, finished cases and questions to review
  • Your settings: age track, country, daily goal, text size and sound
  • The name and organisation you type on your certificate, if any

We never receive this information. Delete it at any time with Agent → Reset progress, or by clearing the site's data in your browser or the app's storage on your phone.

Third-party services

  • Hosting. The website runs on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel processes basic request data such as IP address to deliver pages and keep the service secure.
  • Fonts. When online, the app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which can see your device's IP address.
  • Links. Source articles and official reporting sites open in your browser and follow their own privacy policies.

Children

Red Flag Run doesn't ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children. Parents and teachers can use the app with children and help them choose a track.

Educational content

Red Flag Run is for education only. It isn't legal, financial or security advice. Hotlines and statistics were checked against public sources and can change, so please confirm with the official site for your country. If you're in danger or have lost money, contact your bank and the police straight away.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
